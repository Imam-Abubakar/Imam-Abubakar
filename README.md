## 👋 hi, i'm imam abubakar

i'm a senior software engineer and founder at [sqaleup inc.](https://sqaleupinc.com), with 8+ years of experience building SaaS platforms, backend systems, developer tools, and production software.

i work across the entire product lifecycle, from system design and architecture to implementation, deployment, and continuous improvement. i've worked on products across commerce, financial infrastructure, enterprise software, and mobile applications, while leading engineering teams and helping turn ideas into working products.

i'm particularly interested in **distributed systems, developer experience, practical AI architecture, infrastructure, and the engineering decisions that make software reliable as it grows.**

i believe good engineering isn't just about getting something to work. it's about building something that can be maintained, extended, and trusted in production.

## 🚀 selected work

### [quotesnap ai](https://sqaleupinc.com/work/quotesnap)
**AI-assisted estimating · SaaS · backend architecture**

an AI-powered estimating platform for exterior cleaning businesses. the system uses job details and image analysis to produce structured information, then applies deterministic pricing rules to generate consistent quotes. it connects estimating with quote delivery, follow-ups, payments, and operational workflows.

**focus:** AI vision, structured outputs, pricing engines, APIs, integrations, and production SaaS architecture.

### [invoice journey](https://sqaleupinc.com/work/invoicejourney)
**fintech · event-driven systems · automation**

an invoicing and collections platform that tracks invoices from creation to payment. it combines invoice lifecycle events, a secure client portal, automated reminders, multi-currency support, and AI-assisted payment estimates.

**focus:** event-driven architecture, billing workflows, secure public links, automation, and payment intelligence.

### [sqaleshop](https://sqaleupinc.com/work/sqaleshop)
**multi-tenant SaaS · e-commerce · mobile-first systems**

a commerce platform built for African SMEs that need a straightforward way to sell online. merchants can manage storefronts, products, inventory, orders, and in-person sales while supporting local payment workflows and mobile-first customer experiences.

**focus:** multi-tenancy, commerce architecture, data isolation, integrations, and scalable product development.
